## 6. App Icon Guidelines
- Size: 512×512px for Play Store, 1024×1024px for App Store
- Design: Orange gradient background (`#E65100` → `#FFB300`), white 🏗️ icon centered
- No text in icon (violates both stores' guidelines)
- Rounded corners handled by stores automatically

## 7. Rating & Review Strategy

### Getting Early Reviews
1. In-app prompt shown after 3rd completed booking (not immediately)
2. Workers prompted after 10th job completed
3. Never ask for 5 stars — ask for "honest review"
4. Respond to every 1-2 star review within 24 hours

### Review Response Templates
```
⭐ Negative review response:
"Hi [Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. Our team has reached out to you via email to resolve this. We take every concern seriously — your feedback makes Kuli better for everyone."

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Positive review response:
"Thank you [Name]! 🙏 We're thrilled that [Worker Name] delivered great work. See you again for your next project!"
```

## 8. Google Play A/B Testing Plan

Test the following elements in Google Play Experiments:
1. **App Icon** — Orange gradient vs Dark gradient
2. **Feature Graphic** — Worker photo vs service icons grid
3. **Short Description** — Benefit-led vs feature-led copy
4. **Screenshots** — Customer flow vs Worker earning highlights first

## 9. Technical ASO Requirements

```
Bundle ID:       com.kuli.app
iOS Target:      16.0+
App Size:        < 50 MB
Age Rating:      4+ (No objectionable content)
Privacy Labels:  Location (precise), Name, Phone (account creation)
```
