Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kuli

❓How do I hire a worker on Kuli?

Download the Kuli app, select a service category (Painting, Plumbing, Electrical, etc.), browse verified workers nearby, and book in under 60 seconds. You'll get real-time tracking and pay via UPI or cash.

🛡️Are all workers on Kuli verified?

Yes. All workers are Aadhaar-verified with identity checks, skill assessments, and customer ratings. Look for the green verified badge on worker profiles for extra trust.

💰What commission does Kuli charge workers?

Kuli charges only 10–15% per booking — much lower than traditional contractors (20–40%). Customers pay no booking fees; pricing is always transparent and shown upfront.

🏙️Which cities is Kuli available in?

Currently live in Ahmedabad and Bengaluru, with rapid expansion to Mumbai, Surat, Chennai, Pune, Hyderabad and 45 more cities planned through 2027.

💳What payment methods are supported?

Kuli supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), cash on delivery, and the Kuli wallet.

🆘What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Kuli offers a 48-hour quality guarantee. If you're not satisfied, file a dispute within 48 hours and our team will arrange a re-service or issue a refund.
